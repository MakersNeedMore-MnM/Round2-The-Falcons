# Phase 20: governed response decision desk

## Delivered

- Console decision desk at `/app/decisions`.
- Review of policy-eligible and policy-blocked response options.
- Explicit approve/reject decisions with required rationale.
- Visible benefit, operational impact, residual risk, and approval requirements.

## Boundary

Approving an option records a governed decision only. It does not execute a response, connect to controls, or bypass multi-operator approval requirements. Cascadia continues to report `executionAuthorized: false`.

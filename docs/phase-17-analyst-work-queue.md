# Phase 17: analyst work queue

## Delivered

- Consolidated console work queue at `/app/work-queue`.
- Prioritized view of open incidents, unreviewed/escalated anomaly findings, and exploitable open vulnerabilities.
- Filter controls and direct links to the corresponding operational workspace.

## Boundary

The queue is read-only decision support built from stored data. It does not fabricate priorities, assign cases, change a finding disposition, or trigger containment.
